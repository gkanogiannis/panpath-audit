Prior-environment record for the reproduction claim in the manuscript.

These three files are the corresponding artifacts from an earlier, independent
execution of the same pipeline on different hardware. They are copied verbatim
and are not regenerated by summarize.py.

  summary.tsv           run-level record of that execution
  macros.tex            the generated LaTeX macros, which carry that run's
                        environment (platform, processor count, thread count,
                        memory budget, toolchain, filesystem types)
  input_checksums.tsv   SHA-256 of every input that execution consumed

The two executions differ in processor count, operating system, kernel,
filesystem, and Rust minor version. Comparing input_checksums.tsv here against
../input_checksums.tsv shows the inputs were byte-identical; comparing
summary.tsv here against ../derived/summary.tsv shows the source and traversal
counts, not-embedded bases, outcome classifications, and exit codes were
identical, with only elapsed time, resident memory, and tracked peak differing.

Per-sequence SHA-256 and BLAKE3 digests and the full base ledgers were also
compared across the two executions and were unchanged for every identifier,
including all 40,970 and 40,971 identifiers of the two HPRC audits. Those
comparisons were made against the raw JSON reports, which are not versioned
because of their size.
